Questions parents ask

Who is it for?

Kids ages 8 to 12. Lessons start with the basics, like how money is earned, and build up to saving, credit, investing, and reading a stock chart.

When and where do classes happen?

Live and online, Tuesdays and Thursdays from 7:00 to 7:50 p.m. ET, starting Tuesday, October 13 for 8 weeks. You'll get the class link after you enroll.

How many spots are there?

We keep classes small so every child gets attention. This cohort is capped at 20 kids, and when spots are gone, enrollment closes until the next cohort.

What if we miss a Tuesday?

Come on Thursday. It's a review and make-up day for that week's lesson, and kids who were there Tuesday can come back for extra practice.

What does the app cost?

The Savvy Squad Academy app is free to try. Every lesson, quiz, and the practice stock market are free.

Is the app safe for kids?

There's no open chat and no ads. Kids can only send friendly pre-written cheers. Anything that costs real money goes through a grown-up check first. We only collect parent information, never kids'.

Does my child need their own phone?

No. The app works in any web browser on a phone, tablet, or computer, and up to 8 kids can share one device with their own players.
